FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a website?

Depending on the plan, our turnaround is 5–15 business days. Simple landing pages can be delivered in as little as 3 days, while complex platforms may take up to 4 weeks.

Can I update the website myself after launch?

Yes! We provide a user-friendly content management system (CMS) that allows you to easily update your website's content, images, and more without any technical skills.

What if I need changes after the project is delivered?

We offer post-launch support and maintenance packages to accommodate any changes or updates you may need. Just reach out to our team, and we'll be happy to assist you!

Do you provide hosting and domain services?

Yes! We can help you set up hosting and register a custom domain. We partner with top hosting providers to ensure your site is fast, secure, and always online.
