About Us

Building the Future of Education

For over three decades, EB Academy has been nurturing minds and shaping futures across Addis Ababa, building a legacy of excellence in education.

E

EB Academy

EB Academy is a premier learning hub designed for ambitious individuals. We bridge the gap between traditional learning and the fast-paced demands of the modern industry. Our holistic approach ensures students develop both academically and personally. Our curriculum is built by experts to ensure that every graduate possesses the confidence and technical prowess to excel in their chosen field. With seven branches across Addis Ababa, we bring quality education closer to every family. From pre-primary to high school, our programs are designed to inspire curiosity, foster critical thinking, and build character. Every student at EB Academy is encouraged to discover their unique potential.
